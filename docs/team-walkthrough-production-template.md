# Hackshop-team hardware walkthrough

Use this checklist for a real, versioned build. A concept tour can explain the plan but cannot satisfy physical-demo or real-walkthrough gates.

## Record before filming

- Build/recipe ID and version; exact device and hardware revision (or unknown).
- Firmware/source commit, tools, owned parts versus new purchases, dated costs and exclusions.
- Primary sources checked, backup/recovery instructions and safety limitations.
- Intended first-success behavior and the acceptance checks actually performed.
- Maker, camera/editing credits and media rights. Do not publish keys or token-bearing logs.

## Capture once, publish two roles

1. Show the actual working result on the assembled device.
2. Show exact parts/tools and explain prerequisites and risks before risky steps.
3. Reach one minimal hardware success, then add the useful behavior.
4. Show a common failure and recovery; do not invent a demonstration or risky command.
5. Run the documented result/reboot checks; distinguish observed results from untested steps.
6. End with source links, version, limitations and a request for maker variations.

Deliver a 15–30-second demonstration (no longer than 45 seconds for featured eligibility) and typically a 3–8-minute instructional walkthrough. They are separate media records. Include a poster, useful captions/transcript and chapter timestamps. Long walkthroughs do not replace short proof demos.

Use relevant real device footage. Renders/simulations may explain connections but must remain labeled. AI may assist scripts, edits and captions; it must not fabricate results. Use an approved ElevenLabs voice for synthetic narration; otherwise use a silent captioned preview or human narration.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Correct parts, hardware/firmware revision and dated bill of materials.
- [ ] Finished-device photo and short real-device demonstration with provenance when physical status is claimed.
- [ ] Captions/transcript and chapter timestamps match the actual footage.
- [ ] Creator/rights approval covers this exact edit and destination.
- [ ] Documentation matches what was tested; remaining gaps stay explicit.
- [ ] Featured promotion additionally passes the interactive assembly and strict proof contract.

Current Phase 0 editorial entries are the desk-orb and reSpeaker recipes. Both remain concepts: this template is not a claim that either has been filmed or physically tested.
