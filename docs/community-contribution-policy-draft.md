# Community contribution policy — owner review draft

Not an active upload policy. Rich community uploads are not open in the Phase 0 release. Existing text-only Ideas continues under its current terms until the reviewed replacement ships.

## Ownership and permission

Creators keep ownership. Proposed terms grant Hackshop non-exclusive permission to host, resize and display submitted work with attribution on the site and in public MCP results. The repository's MIT code license does not automatically apply to community images, prose or designs. Code and design files keep their declared licenses.

Off-site promotional reuse, derivative video clips and substantial editorial adaptations require a separate opt-in. A link to a social post is a source, not proof of ownership or permission to rehost it. An agent cannot accept a rights declaration for someone merely because it found the material online.

## Accurate presentation

Keep maker attribution, claimed progress, reviewed evidence and featured status separate. Team submissions have no evidence exemption. A product photo, render, generated image, emulator or successful compilation is not a working physical build.

Ideas and works in progress are welcome. A claim that a finished build works must describe the tested behavior and preserve unknowns. A photo can show assembly, not operation. Stronger evidence and version-matched reproduction are separately reviewed.

## Safety and privacy

Contributors must be entitled to share the material and should remove tokens, Wi-Fi credentials, private screens, identifying home details and unnecessary serial numbers before upload. Record permission for identifiable people. Do not post other people's private information, unlawful material or dangerous unsupported instructions.

Moderators review rights, content and evidence separately from file processing. Scanning does not make a claim true. Images remain private until processing and review pass.

## Proposed controls and retention

Owner preview and explicit confirmation apply to the exact submitted revision and media. Changes require re-review. Makers can edit, withdraw and request removal. Publishing a public copy must not reveal private saved-build notes, checklists or inventory.

Proposed retention: unattached quarantine 24 hours after accounting for upload-token validity; inactive drafts expire after a warning at day 23 and expiry at day 30; rejected originals expire after 7 days; sanitized draft assets follow draft retention; minimal moderation audit 90 days unless a documented hold applies. Account deletion/export and privacy reports need operational coverage before opening uploads.

Removal disables new origin access after the visibility transaction commits; public cache lifetime must be no more than 60 seconds with no stale serving. Already-downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Internal retention, if any, is separate from public visibility and must be disclosed.

## Approval checklist before opening uploads

- [ ] Operator approves final contribution terms and privacy updates.
- [ ] Accountable moderator and backup/urgent-removal coverage are assigned.
- [ ] Real owner/reviewer/cross-account upload lifecycle and withdrawal canaries pass.
- [ ] Storage, retention jobs, persistent quotas and direct-RPC moderation bypass defenses are verified.
- [ ] User-facing notices distinguish upload, submission, approval and live publication.

This is an implementation-policy proposal, not legal advice or a substitute for the operator's review.
